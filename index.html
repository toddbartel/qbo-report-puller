QBO Report Puller

Gislason Targownik Peters LLP

QBO Report Puller is a small Windows desktop application used internally at Gislason Targownik Peters LLP. It connects to QuickBooks Online, pulls a standard set of accounting reports for a chosen date range, and saves them locally as Excel workbooks or PDFs.

This is internal firm software. It is not distributed publicly and is not available for download from this site. This page exists to host the application's authorization callback and its legal terms.

What it does

  • Connects to one QuickBooks Online company per user, via Intuit's OAuth 2.0.
  • Pulls Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Trial Balance, A/R and A/P Aging, Bank Reconciliation, and General Ledger reports.
  • Writes the results to a folder the user chooses, as .xlsx or .pdf.
  • Reads only. It does not create, edit, or delete anything in QuickBooks.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. The application runs entirely on the user's own computer and talks directly to Intuit's API. There is no server, no hosted component, and no analytics. Report data and access tokens never leave the machine the app is installed on. See the Privacy Policy for detail.
