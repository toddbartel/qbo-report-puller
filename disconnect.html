Disconnecting QBO Report Puller

QuickBooks Online

You can revoke QBO Report Puller's access to your QuickBooks Online company at any time. There are two ways, and either one is sufficient.

From the application

  1. Open QBO Report Puller.
  2. Click Disconnect in the connection panel at the top.

This revokes the tokens with Intuit and clears them from your computer immediately.

From QuickBooks Online

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear icon) → Apps.
  3. Open the Connected apps tab.
  4. Find QBO Report Puller and choose Disconnect.

Removing local data

Disconnecting clears the stored tokens. To remove everything else the application saved on your computer — your preferences and report selections — delete this folder:

%APPDATA%\QBOReportPuller

Reports you have already generated are ordinary files in whichever folder you chose; delete them yourself if you no longer need them.

Disconnecting does not delete anything in QuickBooks Online. The application only ever reads from your company file.
