Privacy Policy

QBO Report Puller

Last updated: September 2, 2026
Published by Gislason Targownik Peters LLP ("we", "us")

1. Summary

QBO Report Puller is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. It communicates directly with Intuit's QuickBooks Online API. We operate no server and receive no data from the application. Your QuickBooks data and your access credentials stay on your machine.

2. What the application accesses

With your authorization, the application reads the following from the QuickBooks Online company you connect:

  • Company name and basic company information.
  • Your chart of accounts (account names, numbers, types).
  • Standard financial reports for the date range you select: Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Trial Balance, A/R Aging, A/P Aging, Bank Reconciliation, and General Ledger.

The application performs read operations only. It does not create, modify, or delete any record in your QuickBooks company.

3. What is stored, and where

Data Location
OAuth access and refresh tokens, connected company id %APPDATA%\QBOReportPuller\user_config.json on your computer
Your preferences (last folder, dates, report selections) The same file
Generated reports The output folder you choose
Intuit application credentials firm_config.json, distributed separately by the firm and stored alongside the application

Report data is never cached or retained by the application beyond the files it writes to the folder you choose.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not transmit your QuickBooks data to us or to any third party.
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, tracking, or crash reporting.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data.
  • We do not display advertising.

The only network destinations the application contacts are Intuit's own endpoints (appcenter.intuit.com, oauth.platform.intuit.com, quickbooks.api.intuit.com, and developer.api.intuit.com) and the authorization callback page hosted at toddbartel.github.io, which receives only the short-lived authorization code Intuit returns during sign-in.

5. Third parties

Your use of QuickBooks Online is governed by Intuit's own privacy policy and terms. The callback page is served by GitHub Pages; GitHub may log standard web request metadata (IP address, user agent) as described in GitHub's privacy statement. No QuickBooks report data is ever sent to that page.

6. Revoking access

You can disconnect at any time from within the application, or from QuickBooks Online under Settings → Apps → Connected apps. See How to disconnect. Disconnecting revokes the tokens and clears them from your computer.

7. Retention and deletion

Because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. To remove everything the application has stored locally, disconnect and then delete the folder %APPDATA%\QBOReportPuller.

8. Security

All communication with Intuit uses HTTPS/TLS. Tokens are stored in your Windows user profile and are protected by your operating system account. The application's Intuit client secret is never embedded in the executable; it is supplied in a separate configuration file controlled by the firm.
