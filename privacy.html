QBO Report Puller

Last updated: September 3, 2026
Gislason Targownik Peters LLP

The short version. QBO Report Puller runs on your own computer and talks directly to Intuit. There is no server in between. We receive no data from it — not your QuickBooks data, not your credentials, not even a record that you ran it.

What it reads

With your authorisation, it reads the following from the QuickBooks Online company you connect: the company name, your chart of accounts, and the standard financial reports for the date range you choose — Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Trial Balance, A/R and A/P Aging, and General Ledger.

It only reads. It cannot create, change, or delete anything in QuickBooks.

What it stores, and where

What Where
Your QuickBooks access tokens and the connected company %APPDATA%\QBOReportPuller\ on your computer, encrypted to your Windows account
Your preferences and report selections The same folder
Error messages, for troubleshooting A log file in that folder. It records what failed, not your report data
The reports themselves Whichever folder you choose when you run it
The firm's Intuit credentials A configuration file installed alongside the application

Because the tokens are encrypted to your Windows account, a copy of that file taken to another computer or another user is useless. Report data isn't cached anywhere — it goes into the files you asked for and nowhere else.

What it never does

  • Send your QuickBooks data to us or to anyone else
  • Collect analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking
  • Sell, rent, or share any data
  • Show advertising

Where it connects

Only to Intuit's own endpoints, and to toddbartel.github.io, which hosts the sign-in confirmation page. That page receives only the short-lived authorisation code Intuit returns during sign-in — never any report data. It is served by GitHub Pages, which logs ordinary web request details such as IP address.

Disconnecting and deleting

Click Disconnect in the application, or in QuickBooks Online go to Settings → Apps → Connected apps. See how to disconnect. To remove everything stored locally, disconnect and then delete the folder %APPDATA%\QBOReportPuller. We hold nothing, so there is nothing for us to delete.
